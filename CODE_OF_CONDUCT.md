# Code of Conduct

Tibu is made for small children, and we want the people building it to be just as
kind to each other. This code is based on the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), in our own words.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in Tibu to be welcoming and free of harassment for everyone,
whatever their age, body, ability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of
experience, education, background, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be friendly and patient, especially with first-time contributors.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Own your mistakes and learn from them.
- Think about what is best for the children who play Tibu and for the community.

## What is not okay

- Insults, put-downs, trolling, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Sharing someone else's private information, such as an address or photos,
  without their explicit permission. This includes photos or details of children.
- Anything else that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a project made
  for young kids.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews)
and whenever someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you see or experience something that breaks this code, contact the maintainers
privately through the repository's security advisory form (see
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)) or by any private contact listed on the maintainers'
profiles. Reports are handled promptly and confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, code and issues that break this
code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone whose behavior is
harmful, depending on how serious it is and whether it is repeated.
